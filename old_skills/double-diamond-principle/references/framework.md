## 2. 四個階段:兩個版本的原文定義

### 版本 A:Framework for Innovation / The Double Diamond 頁(現行說法)

出處:<https://www.designcouncil.org.uk/resources/framework-for-innovation/>、<https://www.designcouncil.org.uk/resources/the-double-diamond/>

> Design Council's Double Diamond clearly conveys a design process to designers and non-designers alike. The two diamonds represent a process of exploring an issue more widely or deeply (divergent thinking) and then taking focused action (convergent thinking).

- **Discover.** The first diamond helps people understand, rather than simply assume, what the problem is. It involves speaking to and spending time with people who are affected by the issues.
- **Define.** The insight gathered from the discovery phase can help you to define the challenge in a different way.
- **Develop.** The second diamond encourages people to give different answers to the clearly defined problem, seeking inspiration from elsewhere and co-designing with a range of different people.
- **Deliver.** Delivery involves testing out different solutions at small-scale, rejecting those that will not work and improving the ones that will.

### 版本 B:History 頁(2003–2004 年團隊最初的定義)

出處:<https://www.designcouncil.org.uk/resources/the-double-diamond/history-of-the-double-diamond/>

- **Discover**: The process starts by questioning the challenge and quickly leads to research to identify user needs.
- **Define**: The second phase is to make sense of the findings, understanding how user needs and the problem align. The result is to create a design brief which clearly defines the challenge based on these insights.
- **Develop**: The third phase concentrates on developing, testing and refining multiple potential solutions.
- **Deliver**: The final phase involves selecting a single solution that works and preparing it for launch.

### 兩個版本的對照

| 階段 | 版本 A 強調 | 版本 B 強調 | 產出 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discover | 理解而非假設;接觸受影響的人 | 質疑挑戰;研究使用者需求 | 對現況與需求的洞察 |
| Define | 可能用不同的方式定義挑戰 | 整理發現;對齊需求與問題 | design brief |
| Develop | 不同的答案;外部靈感;共同設計 | 發展、測試、精煉多個可能的解法 | 多個候選解法 |
| Deliver | 小規模測試;淘汰與改進 | 選出一個可行的解法並準備推出 | 選定並驗證過的解法 |

兩個版本不衝突:版本 B 把「測試」放在 Develop,版本 A 把「小規模測試」放在 Deliver —— 這也反映出測試其實貫穿第二顆鑽石。

History 頁對圖形的描述:兩個鑽石由左至右,從最初的挑戰或問題陳述開始,經過中央「要處理的問題的定義」,到右邊的解法。鑽石的形狀代表發散與收斂思考如何落在各階段;各階段中的符號代表研究、學習、原型與測試的迭代點。

團隊成員 Anna White 的說法(History 頁):

> "For me the first part of the diamond is about questioning the brief and defining the problem statement. I explain it as 'designing the right thing'. The second part of the diamond is about exploring possibility, iteration, testing and developing, so 'designing the thing right'."

## 3. 非線性

出處:<https://www.designcouncil.org.uk/resources/framework-for-innovation/>

> This is not a linear process as the arrows on the diagram show. Many of the organisations we support learn something more about the underlying problems which can send them back to the beginning. Making and testing very early stage ideas can be part of discovery. And in an ever-changing and digital world, no idea is ever 'finished'. We are constantly getting feedback on how products and services are working and iteratively improving them.

三個要點:

1. 對根本問題有新的認識,可能把你送回起點。
2. 製作並測試非常早期的想法,可以是 Discover 的一部分。
3. 沒有任何想法是「完成」的;持續收到回饋並迭代改進。

The Double Diamond 頁說明,正是這幾點被納入擴充後的 Double Diamond,成為 Framework for Innovation。

## 4. 四項設計原則

出處:<https://www.designcouncil.org.uk/resources/framework-for-innovation/>

> The framework for innovation outlines four core principles for problem-solvers to adopt so that they can work as effectively as possible.

1. **Put people first.** Start with an understanding of the people using a service, their needs, strengths and aspirations.
2. **Communicate visually and inclusively.** Help people gain a shared understanding of the problem and ideas.
3. **Collaborate and co-create.** Work together and get inspired by what others are doing.
4. **Iterate, iterate, iterate.** Do this to spot errors early, avoid risk and build confidence in your ideas.

## 5. Methods bank

出處:<https://www.designcouncil.org.uk/resources/framework-for-innovation/>

Design Council 撰寫、改編或採用了一組設計方法,分成三個領域,幫助人們運用設計流程去探索、塑形或建造:

| 領域 | 對象 |
|---|---|
| **Explore** | challenges, needs and opportunities(挑戰、需求與機會) |
| **Shape** | prototypes, insights and visions(原型、洞察與願景) |
| **Build** | ideas, plans and expertise(想法、計畫與專業能力) |

該頁面只列出這三個分類,沒有列出個別方法。

## 6. 成功的文化

出處:<https://www.designcouncil.org.uk/resources/framework-for-innovation/>

原文指出,和流程與原則同樣重要的是組織的文化,以及它如何與民眾和夥伴連結:

- **Leadership**:需要領導來鼓勵創新、建立技能與能力、允許實驗與學習。強的領導也讓專案可以保持開放與敏捷,沿途展示成果,並且能夠改變方向。
- **Engagement**:需要和交付這些想法的人、接收這些想法的人,以及可能有其他想法的夥伴互動。建立連結與關係,和產生想法同樣重要。

## 7. Double Diamond 的用途

出處:<https://www.designcouncil.org.uk/resources/the-double-diamond/history-of-the-double-diamond/>

History 頁列出它今天被使用的方式:

- 初步評估專案的類型,以及處理特定挑戰所需的做法
- 促成對多個專案的討論,協助決定優先順序
- 在專案開始時幫助團隊聚焦
- 開始形塑專案的策略與管理
- 檢查專案「現在走到流程的哪裡」
- 幫助人們在兩個發散階段(Discover 與 Develop)自在地「放寬、不聚焦」
- 形塑研究

## 8. 起源與歷史

出處:<https://www.designcouncil.org.uk/resources/the-double-diamond/history-of-the-double-diamond/>

- 2003 年,Design Council 推廣策略性地運用設計,卻沒有標準的方式描述背後的流程。時任 Director of Design and Innovation 的 Richard Eisermann 問團隊:"How do we describe design process?"
- 團隊(Anna White、Chris Vanstone、Gill Wildman、Jennie Winhall、Jonathan Ball 等)回顧 Design Council 各計畫近期的專案,拆解各專案使用的方法,比對出共同的模式,歸納成四個階段,並刻意取了好記的名字。
- 2004 年開始在研討會與簡報中對外分享。
- 鑽石形的流程模型並非首創。Eisermann 的說法:團隊做的是 "codify it, rename the steps and popularise it",並且 "standing on the shoulders of giants";1960 至 1990 年代已有學者提出包含發散、收斂、循環與迭代結構的模型。
- 初衷(Anna White):讓設計流程不再神祕,讓政策制定者、製造業主管、技術人員與教育者等非設計背景的人也能理解。
- The Double Diamond 頁引用的一句話(該頁署名 Tim Browne, Co-Chair at IDEO):"It's not an instruction manual on how to design, it's an invitation to get involved."

## 9. Systemic Design Framework

出處:<https://www.designcouncil.org.uk/resources/systemic-design-framework/>

- 為處理重大而複雜、牽涉不同領域與部門的挑戰而發展。
- 保留 Double Diamond 的核心前提(發散與收斂思考),把四個動作稱為 **Explore、Reframe、Create、Catalyse**。
- 把設計流程擴大到涵蓋周圍的「看不見的活動」:Orientation and vision setting、Connections and relationships、Leadership and storytelling、Continuing the journey。
- 另含六項原則與四種角色(Systems Thinker、Connector and Convener、Designer and Maker、Leader and Storyteller),以及含 11 項工具的 Systemic Design Toolkit。
- 該頁說明 Framework for Innovation 仍是任何在組織內進行創新的人的必備指引。

一般的功能開發用不到這一層;遇到跨組織、牽涉結構性因素的問題時再參考。

## 10. 原文沒有說的事

為了避免把詮釋當成出處,以下幾點**官方頁面沒有規定**,SKILL.md 中對應的內容是為軟體開發所做的轉譯:

- 每個階段在軟體開發中的具體動作(讀程式碼、寫 spike、寫測試等)。
- Develop 階段要提出幾個做法。原文只說 "different answers" 與 "multiple potential solutions"。
- brief 應包含哪些欄位。原文只說它 "clearly defines the challenge based on these insights"。
- 階段之間是否需要使用者確認。原文強調 collaborate 與 co-create,但沒有規定檢查點。
- 小型或緊急工作可以簡化到什麼程度。原文只說這不是操作手冊。
